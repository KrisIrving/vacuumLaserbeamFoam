At 182 us local maximum absolute differences include T 832.518 K (cell-unweighted RMS 2.875 K), epsilon1 1, alpha.metal 0.5372, U component 14.222 m/s, p_rgh 164.342 kPa, Deposition 1.10998e16 and rayQ 47.0623. The difference between global Tmax values is only 0.963 K and does not demonstrate local equivalence. These statistics alone do not locate differences in metal/interface/gas or determine whether they are physically tolerable.

Decision: retain the original partition and the previously validated traversal cache; keep seed shortcut off. Do not promote rayQ-weighted partition, relax gates, repeat this pair, or start fine/full-track production from this result. The weight is a power-path proxy, not measured cell search cost; this test rejects this weighting/decomposition combination, not every possible load-balancing method.

Next development priority is a fixed-state optical regression across decompositions: hold geometry, alpha, temperature and optical properties fixed, execute the same rays without advancing flow/thermal fields, reconstruct deposition/rayQ and compare energy and spatial distributions. This isolates ray ownership, boundary crossing, local stepping and merge/order effects from evolving CFD and parallel linear solves. The present coupled pair does not establish the unique cause of the field differences. Require that evidence before changing the ray exchange backend or resuming partition sweeps. In parallel with the design work, retain moving local CFD coupled to a coarse global thermal field as the full-track acceleration direction. No further user CFD run is requested by this review checkpoint.
